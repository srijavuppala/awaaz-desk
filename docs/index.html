Why this can work

Small businesses lose real money when calls are missed.

The first version is intentionally narrow: inbound answering, customer-requested callbacks, reminders, and simple lead capture. It is not a mass outbound calling tool. We start with one script, one business, and daily review until the call quality is reliable.

Starter pilot

A practical pilot, not a big software contract.

We set up a limited voice flow, review calls, and improve the script with you. The goal is simple: find out whether an AI receptionist saves enough time or recovers enough enquiries to be useful.

Honest limits

Some calls should still go to a person.

A good voice agent should know when to stop. In the pilot, we keep the scope small and make escalation obvious, especially for urgent, sensitive, or unclear conversations.

We do not do this yet

  • No spam calling campaigns
  • No medical, legal, or financial advice
  • No fully unsupervised pilot in week one
  • No replacement for urgent human judgment

Compliance-first

Outbound needs consent and the right calling setup.

Awaaz Desk is designed for customer-requested callbacks, transactional reminders, and registered communication workflows. Promotional outbound calling in India needs careful DND, consent, and telemarketer compliance before scale.
